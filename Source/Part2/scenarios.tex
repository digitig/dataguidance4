\chapter{Scenarios\index{Scenario|textbf}} \label{bkm:scenario}

\dsiwgSectionQuote{Scenarios help solve problems differently because they can illuminate new possibilities and ignite hope.}{Roger Spitz}

Table \ref{tab:Scenarios} section identifies several scenarios under which it might be useful to recognise data as safety significant.

The levels of necessary assurance given in \hl{???Insert reference}
depend on the relevant scenario, and these scenarios
are also used in subsequent chapters to indicate the relevance of
different tools and techniques. 
\begin{longtable}{|C{\dsiwgColumnWidth{0.06}}|L{\dsiwgColumnWidth{0.17}}|L{\dsiwgColumnWidth{0.77}}|}
	\caption{Data Safety Scenarios}
	\label{tab:Scenarios}
	\\\hline\TableHeadColourC{No.} & \TableHeadColour{Scenario} & \TableHeadColour{Description} \\\hline
	\endfirsthead
	\caption[]{Data Safety Scenarios (continued)}
	\\\hline\TableHeadColourC{No.} & \TableHeadColour{Scenario} & \TableHeadColour{Description} \\\hline
	\endhead
	\multicolumn{3}{r}{\sl Continued on next page}
	\endfoot\endlastfoot
	\hline
	S-0 & Check & New or changed data may affect a system and the impact is to be checked. This could be a regular check or ad-hoc.\\
	\hline
	S-1 & New Dataset & A new dataset for a system is to be created from scratch.\\
	\hline
	S-5 & New Use & The way the data is used has changed and an impact analysis needs to be undertaken.\\
	\hline
	S-3 & Restructuring & A dataset for a system is to be restructured, reformatted or otherwise reworked.\\
	\hline
	S-4 & Assurance & An assurance position for data used or produced by a system is to be created to address safety concerns.\\
	\hline
	S-2 & Assembly & A new dataset for a system is to be created from existing data sets.\\
	\hline
	S-6 & Degradation & The data for a system is reduced in quantity or quality, or the system itself operates in a reduced manner and the impact of this needs analysis.\\
	\hline
	S-7 & Investigation & A safety incident related to data has occurred and the cause of this needs to be understood and mitigations proposed if necessary to reduce the risk in the future.\\
	\hline
	S-8 & Migration & Data is to be migrated from one system to another or one data set to another. This may involve re-formatting, transformations, mappings or other changes.\\
	\hline
	S-9 & Cleansing & A dataset is to be cleaned to remove old, obsolete, incorrect or other unwanted data.\\
	\hline
	S-10 & Audit & A dataset is checked for certain aspects on a sampling basis. \\
	\hline
	S-11 & Selection & A dataset is to be filtered or selected to produce a new subset of data.\\
	\hline
	S-12 & Curation & A dataset is to be formally managed and change controlled.\\
	\hline
	S-13 & Identification of Missing & Missing or obscured parts of the data sets are to be identified.\\
	\hline
	S-14 & Archival & A dataset is to be copied to long-term storage and possibly removed from current use.\\
	\hline
\end{longtable}
